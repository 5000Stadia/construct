## CLEANEST PROSE (positive baseline — tic-free, excellent passages)

**1. Nell's witness statement, bodycase Turn 3:**
> "I did not see what Mr. Hobb had, miss. That is the truth. I heard Mr. Liddell say, 'If he brings it to my scales, I am done,' or near enough those words. He did not name the thing. He was by the warehouse gate when he said it, with one hand on the latch, and Mr. Hobb was farther off, coming up from Bluegate way."

Plain, economic, fear-true. No hedged emotion-label, no environmental punctuation, no epigram. The terror is in the syntax's refusal to elaborate.

**2. The scree-fall on Mount Veyr, emberroad Turn 12:**
> "Then the slope gives. Not under you, but beneath the path you left: a run of stones breaks loose and goes rattling down the mountain, loud in the night, iron-hard against the frozen ground. When the noise thins, part of the goat path below is buried under fresh scree. The way back is no longer clean. A slate edge has also torn one boot at the outer sole; cold wet begins to creep in with each shift of weight."

Action mechanics rendered with cost-and-consequence precision; no body-beat, no portentous abstraction, no moral summary. The cold enters the boot and that is enough.

**3. The hardline diagnostic report, thedeep Turn 17 (first paragraph only):**
> "The hardline takes the plug with a dry click. The soft reader lied around the brownout; the hardline does not. Lower ladder contacts are wet and jammed by a fallen panel. The maintenance crawl is passable. Persephone's hiss is regulator trim, not a cabin bleed: ugly, loud, survivable. Chen's last tag is still inside the ascent sphere, and Orpheus has forty-seven minutes by the honest count, less only if someone wastes them."

Each clause delivers a new fact. No atmosphere-padding, no epigram until the last clause, and even that clause earns its place by naming the stakes. The second paragraph of this same turn falls into Theme-as-Epigram (tic 5); the distinction is precise.
