## I. Engine strengths (defend these)

1. **The interview/delivery spine is the engine's crown.** 8 clues in bodycase, 6 in emberroad,
   3 each in thedeep/hedgetest — distinct, earned, advancing, correctly gated. No other system
   I know of makes "questioning a witness" this mechanically real.
2. **Fail-forward resolution drama.** Every drawn tier produced consequence-rich fiction
   (emberroad's scree collapse; thedeep's snapped knife that ESCALATED the station). The
   pre-rolled deck is invisible and superb.
3. **Improv discipline.** Ordinary detail granted generously (kettles, stamp pads, service
   panels); fiat and anachronism denied cleanly; concealment deflected probes without stonewalling
   (adj=deflect worked in-world).
4. **The grounded open** (this week's work): identity, relationships, why-you're-here all landed
   in every post-fix opening.
5. **The conclusion pipeline CAN produce greatness** — bodycase's staged commitment rendered an
   arrest scene with cast fates that satisfies every demand the founder made of endings. The
   machinery exists; it's the ROUTING into it that fails (see weaknesses).
6. **Diegetic instrumentation** (thedeep): gauges as fiction, evidence in machinery — a house
   signature worth spreading to other genres.

## II. Engine weaknesses (ranked by experience cost)

1. **The conclusion is the least protected, most variable surface** (three-way evidence):
   hedgetest's climax CRASHED on a PB bookkeeping raise (#89, HIGH); the founder's unstaged
   wrong accusation got an answer-key dump + a "won" mislabel + a falsified continuation (#88 D/E);
   only the fully-staged path got the great ending. The best moment of a story is currently a
   lottery. → **#89 fail-open + #88 clarify/stage/dramatize are the top builds.**
2. **The world has no initiative.** Anchor's morning that never comes; emberroad's abandoned
   witness pool that never reacts; thedeep's captain who never radios. Every plateau in 90 turns
   is this one gap. → **#84.**
3. **Prose-state capture is unreliable** (nondeterministic extraction): narrated gifts vanish
   (anchor's "office stock" denial), companions ping-pong (the Reed saga), arrivals are ghosts.
   Interim seams shipped (dismissals, companions, seek-unwrap, conjure-ban); the permanent seam
   is #80/#82 + PB's extractor ruling.
4. **Cast identity is under-seeded:** Tin Ear's oscillating gender (#87); "His Enormous Head" as
   a name; Veyr person/mountain collision (#56). Build-side authoring gaps that render can't
   fully paper over.
5. **Session-scale memory is the player's problem:** 8 clues + artifacts held only in the
   agent's context; humans across sessions lose the thread. → **#83.**

## III. Player experience (the felt arc)

- **Openings grip.** Post-fix cold opens ground identity, relationships, stakes — the best
  first-five-minutes in the suite's history.
- **The interrogation loop is the gameplay.** When cast is present, every scenario sings.
- **Solo stretches sag** (all five runs): the moment the player leaves people behind, pacing
  flattens to poke-and-describe. The world must reach in (#84/#85) or pull back (#88 staging).
- **Waits are now labeled** (tonight's pings) — no more dead chat during builds/openings.
- **Endings decide everything.** A player forgives 17 mediocre turns for bodycase's turn 18;
  the founder wiped an entire playthrough over a bad close. The close is where trust lives.
