# World Laws — universal dynamics as first-class canon

**Status:** BUILT (task #105; Cx 470 YELLOW → rulings accepted 471 → foundation
GREEN 472/473; suite 804). See **As built** at the end for the shipped shape,
including the founder's two post-spec refinements (the interview's
universe-details beat and the per-law DISCLOSURE axis).
**Provenance:** the founder's Star Wars question — "what are the odds a similarly
intended sci-fi fantasy world establishes unique world elements as universe-affecting
as the Force, Tatooine's politics, Jedi/Sith, the Empire?" — plus his two refinements:
(1) domain respect ("builds that would equate to A Few Good Men, Pelican Brief,
Sleepless in Seattle must NOT create 'the Force'"), and (2) the reality register
(real-world vs. fictional-world vs. alternate-Earth) as an explicit interview and
build dimension alongside location and time period.

---

## The diagnosis

The build authors SETTING (places, cast, situation), PREMISE, TONE, and a SHAPE OF
PLAY (the 155 cards). Nothing ever demands a **named, novel, universe-governing
dynamic** — so an "epic sci-fi fantasy, surprise me" build fills the vacuum with the
model's prior: Force-adjacent pastiche, generic empires, trope-average texture. The
murder-mystery default, one level up.

What makes an owned universe (Star Wars, Middle-earth, Hogwarts) is not texture but a
**causal stack**: a governing dynamic with rules and costs → institutions that exist
BECAUSE of it → politics entangled with it → artifacts, rituals, and language derived
from it. Vanilla worlds have the texture without the stack because nobody asked the
builder for a stack.

**Why this engine specifically should do it:** binding constraints are the house
specialty. Canon that can't be contradicted, protected keys, the narrator leash,
adjudication that refuses impossible actions, and PINNED UNIVERSALS (awareness
element 1 — the standing-facts channel the narrator holds every turn) already exist.
The ENFORCEMENT rail is built; the build never authors a CONSTITUTION for it to
enforce. This spec is that constitution stage. For the player, the payoff is the
founder's fan-fiction property: playing in a universe whose laws you must respect —
except the universe has never existed before, and the engine is the keeper of the
respect.

## The law object

Each world authors **1–4 laws** (count scaled by ambition — see intensity). A law is
five mandatory parts:

| Part | What it is | Star Wars calibration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **NAME** | proper, ownable, speakable in-world — never "the magic" | the Force |
| **RULE** | what it makes possible/impossible, concretely | the attuned sense and move what others cannot; it binds all living things |
| **COST / LIMIT** | what it demands — a law without a price is a superpower | attunement takes formation; power pulls toward corruption (the dark side) |
| **EMBODIMENT** | who/what institutionalizes it — cast and politics get built ON this | Jedi and Sith orders; the Senate/Empire axis entangled with both |
| **TEXTURE** | the visible derivatives: artifacts, rituals, oaths, slang | lightsabers; "may the Force be with you" |

Three calibration exhibits spanning the registers (these go in the authoring prompt):

- **Invented** (secondary world): the Force — see table above.
- **Sharpened-real** (A Few Good Men): NAME "the Code" · RULE unit-corps-God-country;
  the informal law outranks the formal one · COST following it can mean crossing the
  law of the land; breaking it means exile from the tribe · EMBODIMENT the chain of
  command, the JAG courts opposing it · TEXTURE "you can't handle the truth,"
  code reds, the crisp rituals of rank. **No invented physics — a real dynamic
  sharpened until it governs this story's universe.**
- **Delta** (alternate Earth, Harry Potter): NAME the Statute of Secrecy · RULE our
  Earth exactly, PLUS a hidden magical layer that must stay hidden · COST living
  double lives; the Ministry's whole apparatus exists to police the seam ·
  EMBODIMENT the wizarding institutions · TEXTURE wands, platform 9¾, owls.
  **The DELTA ITSELF is authored as a law: what differs from real Earth, and every
  consequence of the difference.**

## Domain respect — the register gate (founder constraint 1)

Laws come in **registers**, and the card families + reality register gate which are
permissible. A courtroom drama's universe is governed as absolutely as Star Wars —
by different KINDS of law:

| Register | What its laws are | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| METAPHYSICAL | invented physics/powers/cosmology | the Force; a god that answers letters |
| SYSTEMIC | institutions, codes, machineries of power | the Code; the courts; the Ministry |
| SOCIAL | the binding customs of a community | the town's fog schedule; who may marry whom |
| ENVIRONMENTAL | the world's physical givens as governors | the ocean's pressure; the winter; the desert crossing |
| DELTA | one authored divergence from real Earth + its consequences | the South won; the dead linger a year |

**Family → default register palette** (the authoring gate; the reality register below
further constrains):

| Family | Default registers | Metaphysical allowed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investigation & Epistemics | SYSTEMIC, SOCIAL | only if the world register invites it |
| Puzzle, Escape & Decoding | SYSTEMIC, ENVIRONMENTAL | if invited |
| Schemes & Infiltration | SYSTEMIC, SOCIAL | if invited |
| Action, Combat & Pursuit | SYSTEMIC, ENVIRONMENTAL | if invited |
| Survival, Scarcity & Endurance | ENVIRONMENTAL, SYSTEMIC | if invited |
| Exploration, Wonder & Place | ENVIRONMENTAL, METAPHYSICAL | yes |
| Social, Relationship & Intimacy | SOCIAL, SYSTEMIC | rarely (a soft "serendipity" law at most — Sleepless in Seattle's destiny is TONE, authored as a quiet SOCIAL law, never a power system) |
| Politics, Factions & Institutions | SYSTEMIC | if invited |
| Horror, Dread & the Uncanny | METAPHYSICAL, ENVIRONMENTAL | yes — but ONE law, held partly hidden (the dread IS the law's edges) |
| Stewardship, Building & Management | SYSTEMIC, ENVIRONMENTAL | if invited |
| Creativity, Craft & Performance | SOCIAL, SYSTEMIC | if invited |
| Professional & Procedural Competence | SYSTEMIC | no by default (A Few Good Men / Pelican Brief live here) |
| Moral, Psychological & Literary Drama | SOCIAL, SYSTEMIC | rarely |
| Comedy, Farce & Chaos | SOCIAL | a single absurd law is the classic engine ("the ghost can only be seen by one man") — permitted deliberately |
| Competition, Status & Proving Grounds | SYSTEMIC, SOCIAL | if invited |
| Mythic, Spiritual & Symbolic | METAPHYSICAL | yes — this family EXPECTS one |
| Time, Reality & Metafiction | METAPHYSICAL | yes — the premise usually IS the law (the loop, the haunting) |
| Communication, Collection & Interpretation | SYSTEMIC, SOCIAL | if invited |
| Transgression, Power & Ensemble Forms | SOCIAL, SYSTEMIC | if invited |

"If invited" = the brief/reality register explicitly opens it (a fantasy heist may
have magic; a Pelican Brief heist must not).

**Intensity scales with ambition:** an epic secondary world authors 3–4 laws with a
metaphysical anchor; a cozy seaside mystery authors 1–2 QUIET laws ("the fog keeps a
schedule, and the town's whole life bends around it") — same mechanism, whisper
volume. The count/registers derive from (reality register × family × brief), never a
fixed quota.

## The reality register (founder constraint 2)

An explicit, load-bearing build dimension, established in the interview alongside
WHERE and WHEN:

| Register | Contract | Improv/adjudication discipline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **REAL WORLD** | actual Earth, actual era; institutions and physics are real | invention stays PLAUSIBLE-REAL (a fictional senator, yes; a fictional federal agency that contradicts the real structure, no; no metaphysical laws — SYSTEMIC/SOCIAL/ENVIRONMENTAL only) |
| **ALTERNATE EARTH** | real Earth PLUS authored deltas | each delta is a LAW (named, ruled, costed); everything NOT covered by a delta defaults to real-Earth truth — the seam is the world's most interesting line and adjudication polices it |
| **SECONDARY WORLD** | invented whole | free within its own laws; the laws are the only Earth it has, so they carry more weight, not less |

Meta fields: `reality_register`, plus the existing location/time-period grounding
(the interview's WHERE/WHEN beats already anchor these; they become explicit meta).
The register feeds the same three consumers as the laws (cast, arc, adjudication) and
gates the register palette above.

## Enforcement wiring (the point of doing this here)

1. **Build stage** ("Authoring the world's deep laws," after theme, before cast): an
   `author_laws` cohort produces the laws against (brief, reality register, family
   palette, intensity); a deterministic lint checks each law has all five parts, its
   register is in the gate, and its NAME passes `is_proper_named`. Committed as canon:
   `law:<slug>` entities (kind=world_law, register, rule, cost, texture) with
   embodiment links to the institutions/cast it shapes.
2. **Pins:** each law's one-line form rides the pin channel — the narrator holds the
   constitution EVERY turn (the existing pinned-universals rail; no new surface).
3. **Cast authoring:** `author_cast` receives THE LAWS block — institutions and
   suspects exist BECAUSE of the laws (the Jedi property); at least one pillar/clue
   per major law where the shape allows.
4. **Arc:** the generator receives the laws; the destination should TURN ON one (the
   climax of a lawful world is a law-moment: the Code confronted, the delta exposed,
   the metaphysic's cost paid).
5. **Adjudication/improv:** the deny/grant judgment reads the pinned laws —
   assured-for-the-attuned, refused-for-others; in REAL register, refuse
   metaphysical player moves honestly; in ALTERNATE, police the seam.
6. **Interview:** WHERE / WHEN / TONE / SPARK gain two beats — **REALITY** ("real
   world as it is, our world with one strange difference, or a world of its own?")
   and, when register+family invite it, **THE LAW** ("shall this world have a strange
   law of its own — a power, a pact, a physics? or shall its rules be the real
   world's, sharpened?"). Surprise-me rolls these with the shape dice.
7. **Anti-trope clause** (the founder's vanilla complaint, generalized): the authoring
   prompt demands "what does THIS universe have that no existing franchise has? A
   borrowed element must be twisted until its CONSEQUENCES change — different
   texture is not different; different consequences are."

## Test bar (sketch, for the build round)
- A Professional & Procedural build (A Few Good Men-shaped brief) authors SYSTEMIC
  laws only — lint rejects a metaphysical law in REAL register.
- A Mythic/secondary build authors a named metaphysical law with all five parts.
- An alternate-Earth build authors its delta AS a law; adjudication refuses a move
  that contradicts un-delta'd real-Earth truth.
- Laws land as canon rows + pins; the cast prompt carries THE LAWS; the arc's
  destination references one.
- A REAL-register build refuses a player's metaphysical improv honestly.

## As built (task #105 — Cx 470 rulings folded)

- **`construct/laws.py`** — the canonical data + pure logic: `FAMILY_PALETTE`
  (all 19 card families → registers + metaphysical policy; a data-drift test
  pins it to the taxonomy), `allowed_registers(families, reality)` (the
  reality-register overlay: REAL strips metaphysical+delta; ALTERNATE adds
  delta over real-Earth discipline; SECONDARY lets the palette decide),
  `lint_laws` (the SHALLOW deterministic checks only — five parts,
  register-in-gate, `is_proper_named`, calibration-copy, texture-only
  consequence, disclosure), and the single-source renders `laws_block`
  (the compact THE LAWS block every consumer shares) / `law_lines` (the
  briefing lane) / `law_rows` / `embodiment_rows`.
- **Authoring** (`cohorts.author_laws`, task ⟦law⟧) runs FIRST on the
  generated/interview paths — the source fiction is written ON the laws
  (the Jedi property), never retrofitted; a real ingested book keeps its own
  implicit constitution (laws skipped). The loop is author → lint →
  `law_critic` (⟦lwc⟧, the semantic anti-pastiche judgment — Cx 470 ruling 2)
  → feedback retry ×2, fail-open to law-less. The author settles an
  undeclared reality register from the brief; the gate re-derives against it.
- **DISCLOSURE (founder refinement):** each law is marked `understood` (the
  player's character inherently knows it — the intro/opening grounding
  introduces it as lived context) or `discovered` ("magic is real and you're
  a wizard" — never stated; the briefing carries it under a STILL
  UNDISCOVERED weave directive, and it still binds adjudication silently).
- **Canon:** `law:<slug>` rows (kind=world_law + name/register/rule/
  cost_limit/texture/disclosure) committed before arc authoring;
  `embodied_by` links written from `author_cast`'s `law_embodiment`
  declarations (ruling 5 — the causal stack is queryable). Meta mirrors
  `laws` + `reality_register` for the turn loop's cheap read.
- **Consumers, one render:** the arc author (destination turns on a law),
  `author_story`/`interview_world` (institutions exist BECAUSE of laws),
  `author_cast` (+ embodiment), `author_intro` (understood laws only), and at
  play time `classify` (assured-for-the-attuned / refused-for-others),
  `equipment_check` (carry + take), and `propose_reshape` (miracles bound by
  law; ALTERNATE seam policed) — all receive the same `laws_block` (ruling 3).
- **The reserved lane (ruling 4):** the narrator briefing renders WORLD LAWS
  ahead of the capped PINS block — proven by an overcrowded-pin test
  (_PIN_CAP+2 active pins, every law still briefed). `TurnTrace.laws` is the
  debug surface.
- **Interview:** `set_reality` tool + a REALITY beat (asked with WHERE/WHEN),
  and THE LAW beat (founder) — when reality+shape invite invention, the guest
  is asked once whether the universe has rules THEY want folded in;
  what they bring rides the brief into `author_laws`.
- Tests: `tests/test_laws.py` (palette/gate/lint/renders/rows/authoring loop)
  + `TestWorldLaws` in `tests/test_integration.py` (lane, disclosure split,
  shared block) + a `_finalize_scenario` seal test + architect reality tests.
