# The Optimal Interactive-Fiction Experience — a synthesis

**Provenance:** founder mandate (2026-07-02): "consider in a generalized sense what the
better architecture or experience is for an optimal interactive fictions experience."
Grounded in the critic campaign's five full runs (eval/09), the eval suite (eval/00–08),
the founder's live sessions, and the probe transcripts — read as experiences, not defect
logs. This is a living document; future play feeds it.

---

## The thesis

An optimal interactive fiction is a world that **remembers, moves, and answers** — and
whose deepest loyalty is to the player's story, not its own. Every failure we have
observed, across ~150 evaluated turns and every founder complaint on record, reduces to
a violation of one of five pillars. Every moment of magic reduces to their alignment.

## Pillar 1 — Truth-keeping (the foundation everything stands on)

**The contract is two-sided: nothing narrated that isn't true (or committed-to-become-
true in the same breath); nothing true that can't eventually be discovered.**

Every hard immersion break in the corpus is a one-sided violation:
- Prose acted where the map never went (thedeep's eight-turn false journey; bodycase's
  remote warehouse inspection; the probe's phantom foreman's office).
- The map held truth the prose denied (Tin Ear present-but-unseen; the "vanishing"
  hatch reader that was real but elsewhere).
- Two truths in one body (Maud chief/costermonger — a build-time violation).

The architecture's core bet — canon substrate, leashed narrator, map-governs — is
CORRECT, and the campaign shows the honest half working even against errors (turn-later
denials are the system *refusing to lie twice*). The residual structural gap is the
**narration seam**: prose that implies state (travel, arrivals, scene furniture) which
never commits. Three of the campaign's five engine fixes were incidence-reducers on
this seam (embedded movement, fixture heads, opening truth-bar); the permanent answer
remains #80's committed narration seam. **This is the single highest-leverage remaining
investment in the engine.** A player forgives a plain sentence; they never forgive the
world contradicting itself about where they stood.

## Pillar 2 — The player's story is the story

**The authored material is fuel, not scripture.** The off-path runs are the proof the
milestone asked for: a romance the story never offered became, in one run, the richest
texture in the suite (the red-thread token, Dove Court, seed-cake), and in the other,
genuine three-way drama — because the world let the authored case PRESS INTO the chosen
tangent as pressure and payoff rather than railroading back to it. The witness's trust,
earned through courtship, is what surfaced the weigh-door clue. That is the optimal
shape: **the authored story competes for the player's attention on merit, woven at
natural seams — it never outranks the player's chosen one.**

Two corollaries the campaign exposed:
- Continuations must carry the player's PERSONAL threads with the same weight as case
  threads (the rail-end promise vanished from ch2's fuel while the case survived —
  F14). Whatever the ledger remembers, the world honors.
- The one sanctioned exception is death in death-endable shapes (founder ruling, #95):
  the player's story can END by their choice — with a testament, not a contortion.

## Pillar 3 — Initiative (a world that moves without you)

The eval's flattest stretches — anchor's morning that never comes, bodycase's six
airless inspection turns — are all one gap: nothing happens that the player didn't
cause. The world-tick (#84) is the architectural answer, with the crucial discipline
that off-screen motion is DISCOVERED (presence and scene truth on return), never
narrated omnisciently. The optimal calibration is modest: a world that visibly got on
with its life while you were away reads as alive; a world that performs events AT you
reads as a script. Watch post-reboot builds for the middle-game sag lifting.

## Pillar 4 — Proportion and earned prose

Replies sized to moves; conversation that advances; craft earned, not performed. The
campaign's cleanest passages (Nell's testimony, the scree-fall, the hardline report)
share one property: **every clause delivers a new fact**. The recurring tics (the
recital, the re-recognition, the verbatim hook, the re-painted tableau) share the
opposite: language moving while the story stands still. The style layer now names this
directly; the deeper guarantee is mechanical (SAY IT ONCE — a proposed hook leaves the
deck; a delivered fact is knowledge, not a lead).

## Pillar 5 — The ending is the payoff, and the player enacts it

Players forgive seventeen mediocre turns for one bodycase turn-18; the close decides
the memory. The two-beat close (reckoning scene → aftermath-on-the-world) is now the
house shape and it generalizes beyond accusations. Two refinements the campaign argues:
- **The player should enact the climax they earned.** Thedeep's world-event terminal
  declared the solution mid-investigation and fast-forwarded the rescue — a beautiful
  close that nonetheless took the final move out of the player's hands. Where a shape
  permits, hold the terminal one beat for the player's own act.
- **No chapter ends invisibly.** The silent conclude (three sightings) leaves the next
  chapter inventing history ("your earlier matter came good"). Every close gets its
  beat — even a soft one; every continuation opens from what truly happened, with
  answered questions retired (the clean-solve re-tread, #96, is what happens
  otherwise).

## Where the architecture stands against the pillars

| Pillar | Serves it today | Fights it / gap |
|---|---|---|
| Truth-keeping | canon substrate, leashed render, presence truth, Entity Authority, adjudication | the narration seam (#80); classify's read of embedded/implied state changes |
| Player's story | improv grant paths, card-weaving governance, companion state, stage-gated conclusions | continuation fuel under-weights personal threads (#96/F14); vocative/title resolution (#93) |
| Initiative | the world-tick (#84), NPC turns, clocks/gauges | tick untested live at scale; per-NPC schedules parked |
| Proportion | PROPORTION/ADVANCES/EARNED clauses, SAY-IT-ONCE mechanics | old-world rebuilds needed for identity anchors (#87 rows) |
| The ending | two-beat close, clarification gate, coverage bounce, doorway, testament ruling (#95) | silent-conclude beat (founder ruling pending); player-enacted world-event climaxes; clean-solve freshness (#96) |

## The generalized statement

If the five pillars had to compress to one sentence for any future engine, host, or
model generation, it is this: **keep a single truth and make everything face it — the
narrator, the cast, the chapters, and the player's own chosen story — so that memory,
motion, and consequence all agree; then spend the entire craft budget on the two moments
that fix the experience in memory: the moment the world first feels lived-in, and the
moment it answers for everything at the end.**
