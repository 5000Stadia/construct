The player draws their own conclusions — let them, even when they are heading somewhere wrong. The truth is told only at the end, never coaxed out mid-play.

MOST turns plant nothing new; add a fresh thread only when the trail has genuinely gone cold, and prefer a DIFFERENT facet over repeating one the player already holds.
