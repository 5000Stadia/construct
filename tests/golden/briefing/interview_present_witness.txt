Never re-surface the SAME clue every turn, and never bend every scene and every character toward it — a prop the world keeps shoving forward, with NPCs twitching at it each time, reads as fixation and breaks the fiction. MOST turns plant nothing new; add a fresh thread only when the trail has genuinely gone cold, and prefer a DIFFERENT facet over repeating one the player already holds.
